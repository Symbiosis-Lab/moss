> Already write on Matters? Bring your posts in, and moss turns them into a website that's yours.

Your writing on Matters becomes your own website. You add Matters once, log in, and moss copies your posts over — cover images, the pictures inside, comment and like counts. Nothing on Matters changes.

## Add Matters

**1. Open the channel list.** In the top-right of the main window is a small **+** button. Hover it to see **Add channel**, then click. moss opens settings on the **Channels** tab.

**2. Add Matters.** Find the **Matters** icon. Hover it — a hint reads **Matters (click to add)**. Click it. The hint flips to **Matters (click to remove)**, confirming it's added.

## Log in

Right after you add Matters, moss opens the real Matters login page in a small window. Log in the way you always do.

After you log in, the window sits still for about twenty seconds. **This is normal.** moss waits, checks for you, and closes the window once it's in. No action needed.

## Your posts come in

moss starts copying your posts. A slim progress line appears near your writing to show progress — watch near the title, not the bottom of the window.

Progress moves through: **Fetching articles from Matters.town...**, **Found N article(s) to sync**, then each post as **Syncing article:** followed by the post's title. The longest stretch is pictures, shown as **Downloading n/m...**. Posts arrive one by one with cover images, inline pictures, and comment and like counts.

Posts land in a folder named **文章** (Articles) if you write in Chinese, or **articles** otherwise.

A few notes:

- The first sync takes a while; moss brings in everything. Later runs are quick — it only checks for what's new or changed.
- Drafts stay on Matters. moss imports published posts only.

## Your site comes together

As posts arrive, moss builds the site and shows it in the right-hand pane. This preview is just for you — nothing is online yet.

## Put it online

**1. Press Publish.** In the top-right is a round button. Hover it to see **Publish**, then click. moss opens a short setup titled **Set up your site**.

**2. Pick your web address and enter your email.** moss suggests an address ending in `.mosspub.com`, like `my-blog`, that you can keep or edit. It checks availability and shows **Available** or **Not available**. Type your email under **Email**, then choose **Verify email**.

**3. Click the link in your email.** moss shows **Check your email** and waits (**Waiting for verification...**). Open the email and click the link. moss notices and moves on. (If you've published with this email before, moss skips this step and the button reads **Publish**.) If the email doesn't arrive, use **Resend** or **Change email**.

**4. You're live.** moss shows **Setting up your site...**, then **Published.** with the line **Your site is live** and your web address. Click **Visit site** to see it, or **Later** to keep writing.

That's it. Press **Publish** any time to bring in newer posts or update your site.
