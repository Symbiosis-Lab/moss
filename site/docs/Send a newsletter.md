> Already have readers? Email your latest post straight to their inbox, from your own site.

A newsletter lets you send a post to readers by email, straight from your site. Turn it on once, add your readers, then send any post you like.

![[email sub.mp4|loop]]

Newsletter sending works for sites published to mosspub.com hosting (provided by Symbiosis Lab). Sites hosted elsewhere cannot send from moss yet.

## Turn on the newsletter

**1. Open Channels.** In settings, open the **Channels** tab.

**2. Add the newsletter.** Find the **Email Newsletter** tile. Click the small **+** badge on its icon to switch it on. The badge flips to a **−**, confirming it's on.

## Add your readers

**1. Open Subscribers.** In settings, open the **Subscribers** tab. You'll see a **Subscribers** heading with a count, and a list of everyone signed up.

**2. Press + Add.** Click **+ Add** to open a short form.

**3. Type the emails.** Under **Emails (one per line)**, paste or type one address per line. If your site has more than one language, you'll pick a **Language** first, so each reader hears from you in the right one. A note reminds you these are **Added without confirmation email.**, so only add people who asked to hear from you.

**4. Press Add.** The form closes, the list refreshes, and the count goes up. If an address can't be added, moss tells you and adds nothing.

Each reader has a status, shown as a small mark you can hover for the word: **Confirmed**, **Unverified**, **Pending**, **Bounced**, **Complained**, or **Unsubscribed**. To remove someone, click the **×** at the end of their row. To keep a copy of your list, use **Export CSV** at the bottom.

## Send a post

**1. Turn on email for the post.** Open the post you want to send. In the publish panel, switch on the newsletter channel for this post. This only arms it — nothing is sent yet. Email is off by default.

**2. Press Publish.** moss puts your latest changes online, then opens the email composer on the left.

**3. Fill in the message.** The composer shows a few rows:

- **From**: your name, sending from your site's address at `your-site@mosspub.com`. If you've set your email in settings, a line confirms replies reach you.
- **To**: the readers who'll get this. On a site with more than one language, pick which group.
- **Subject**: the line readers see first. Type one under **Enter a subject**.
- **Preview**: an optional **Short hook shown below the subject line in inboxes**.

The body starts from your post and can be edited freely. Changes are saved as a draft and never touch the published post.

**4. Send a test first.** Click **Send test** to mail a copy to yourself. (Set your email in settings to use this.)

**5. Send it.** Click **Send → N**, where N is how many readers will get it. moss shows a recap, **Send to N subscribers?**, so you can check the From, To, Subject, and Preview one last time. Press **Send now → N** to send, or **Go back** to keep editing.

## What you'll see

When it's on its way, a message confirms the send and the composer closes. Your draft clears. Your readers have the post in their inbox; your site stays as it is.
