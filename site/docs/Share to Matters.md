> Wrote something new? Send it to your Matters profile too, and keep a link back to your own site.

![[publish-fanout.html]]

This page shares a single new post back to Matters, so readers there see it too, while the original stays on your site. It works for one post at a time — whichever you are looking at in preview.

## Share a post

**1. Open the post.** Click the post so you can see it in preview. Sharing is only available while a post is open. If something else is open, the Matters button is greyed out with the note **Navigate to an article to syndicate**.

**2. Turn on Matters.** In the publish panel, switch on the **Matters.Town** channel for this post. This only arms it — your next publish will share the post. Nothing is sent on its own.

**3. Press Publish.** Click **Publish**. moss puts your latest changes online, then prepares a Matters draft of this post and opens it in your browser for you to look over.

**4. Publish on Matters.** Review the draft on Matters and publish it there. moss waits in the background. (moss never publishes to Matters for you.) When you publish, moss adds a link back to the post on your own site, connecting the two.

## What you'll see

When the post is shared, a message appears: **Syndicated N article(s) to Matters**, with a **View profile** button that opens your Matters profile.

If you close the Matters draft without publishing, moss saves it and leaves a reminder — **Draft saved, publish it on Matters when ready** — with an **Open draft** button to return later.

If there's nothing new to share, moss shows **No new articles to syndicate** and does nothing. Already-shared posts are skipped, so pressing Publish again won't create duplicates.

## First time? Sign in

The first time, moss asks you to **Sign in to Matters to syndicate your posts**. Choose **Sign in** and connect your account once.
