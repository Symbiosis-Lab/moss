The moss app is free to download and use without a plan. A plan is for publishing your site with us: we serve it, back it up and send its email. Prices are in US dollars, plus applicable tax.

:::grid 3 {rows=equal}
### Basic

**$12 a month, or $120 a year**

Your site served over HTTPS and backed up, with its video and email sending, for a site you edit yourself.

+++
### Business

**$35 a month, or $350 a year**

Everything in Basic, plus booking, payments and memberships.

+++
### Care

**$149 a month, on top of Basic or Business**

About one hour a month of content updates, style updates and fixes, with a 2-business-day response. Nonprofits and education: 20% off Care, once we have verified them.

[Talk to us about Care](mailto:guo@symbiosis-lab.org?subject=moss%20Care)
:::
