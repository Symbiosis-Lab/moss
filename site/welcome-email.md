Hi,

I'm Guo Liu, and I build moss. Thanks for signing up, I'm glad you're here.

moss is a free, open-source app for writing and previewing your own website on your own computer. Your site is a folder of plain files that you own and can take anywhere. There's no platform in the middle.

Publishing is a separate, optional step. Symbiosis Lab, where I work, provides hosting: one site at a `yourname.mosspub.com` address, free for six months. After that, point your own domain at it, or host the files wherever you like.

If you haven't yet, [download moss](https://mosspub.com) and try it on a folder of markdown files. It's early, so you'll hit rough edges, and I'd like to hear about them.

Questions, bugs, or just what you hope to make: just reply to this email. It reaches me.
